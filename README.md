5 Major Signs Your Ductless AC Needs Repair

As your ductless AC transitions into spring use, warning signs can start to appear after months of inactivity. Recognizing these issues early helps prevent breakdowns and keeps your system running efficiently as temperatures rise.

1. Weak or Uneven Airflow

Inconsistent airflow may be caused by filthy filters, blocked vents, or internal component issues.

2. Unusual Noises

Clicking, buzzing, or rattling sounds often indicate loose parts or electrical problems that need immediate attention.

3. Warm Air Instead of Cool Air

If your system is not cooling properly, it may be due to low refrigerant levels or compressor issues.

4. Higher Energy Bills
   
A sudden spike in energy costs may mean your system is working harder than necessary due to inefficiency.

5. Water Leaks or Moisture Build-Up

Leaks around the indoor unit can result from clogged drain lines or frozen coils. These may lead to further damage if ignored.
